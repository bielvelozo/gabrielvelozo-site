01 Fashion · Brazil

arara-demo.myshopify.com
Watch · 0:24

Video, no soundPortuguese · reais

A fashion store ready to sell in Brazil

Brazilian shoppers check the Pix price, the installments, the shipping to their postcode and their size before anything else. Most themes show none of it, so stores stack paid apps and slow down.

  • The Pix price and “6x interest-free” on every price
  • Shipping quoted by postcode on the product page and in the bag
  • A size finder that turns measurements into a size
  • Photos for each color, quick add, shop-the-look and back-in-stock requests

For your store: shoppers see what they'll pay, the shipping and their size before checkout, with no monthly app behind it, and you edit all of it in the Shopify admin.

Watch · 0:25

Video, no soundEnglish · US dollars

A skincare store that sells the routine, not one bottle

Skincare shoppers buy a routine, and few know which three products to pick. Stores answer with a bundle app, a quiz app and a reviews app, each with its own bill and its own look.

  • Any three products make a routine, with 15% off from a Shopify automatic discount
  • A five-question skin quiz that picks three products and says why
  • Product pages with the texture, the hero ingredient, shades and a before-and-after slider
  • A journal that sells the products it mentions

For your store: bigger orders without a bundle app. The discount, the quiz answers, the shades and the reviews are fields in the admin, not code.

03 Fine jewelry · 3D

nocte-demo.myshopify.com
Watch · 0:20

Video, no soundthree.js · WebGL

Fine jewelry you can turn in your hand

Jewelry sells on detail and trust. Shoppers want to see the stone up close, in the metal they picked, and to know the ring will fit.

  • Every piece modeled in 3D, with a diamond shader that bends light inside the stone
  • Metal and stone change with the variant; drag to turn it and read the engraving inside the band
  • A ring size guide, gift wrap with a card, consultation booking and AR on iPhone
  • A jeweler's-glass layer in WebGL: a loupe over the product cards and the name cast in gold

For your store: shoppers see the exact piece they're buying, so fewer stop to ask and fewer rings come back. The 3D only loads after the first interaction.

Customers see their gift before they pay

Personalized gifts sell on trust. The customer wants to see their name or photo on the mug before paying, and the shop needs to know exactly what to print.

  • Text and photo previewed live on a mug, tee or tote, in 2D or 3D
  • A warning before checkout when a photo would print blurry
  • A cart drawer with a free-shipping ribbon and one-click add-ons
  • A speed pass and an app that syncs orders with an ERP, both further down the line

For your store: the customer buys what they saw, and the order arrives with the text, the font, the photo and a preview image.

06 Cart & checkout

Where the sale is won or lost

The cart is the last stop before checkout. It should show the real total and the shipping, say what's left for free shipping and let the shopper add one more thing without leaving.

  • Prices the way your shoppers pay: Pix and installments in Brazil, a bundle discount in the bag
  • Free-shipping progress, one-click add-ons and gift wrap with a card
  • Discounts that are real Shopify discounts, so the number in the cart is the number at checkout
  • On Shopify Plus, checkout extensions: a gift option, a survey on the Thank-you page, delivery rules with Functions
Cart

Arara. In the bag: how much is left for free shipping, the Pix price, the installments and the shipping to the shopper's postcode.

Dewlane. Pick any three and the 15% comes off in the bag, no code to type.

Nocte. Gift wrap and a handwritten card, chosen in the bag and saved to the order.

Inkly. A cart drawer with a free-shipping ribbon that ties a bow, and add-ons in one click.
